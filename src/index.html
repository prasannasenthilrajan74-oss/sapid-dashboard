Privacy Policy

SAPID license analyser Data Protection

1. Local-First Architecture

SAPID license analyser is designed to be a local-first application. All processes run locally on your system. None of your data, sheet configurations, or uploaded spreadsheets are sent to external trackers or analytics servers.

2. Google Sheets & Apps Script Sync

When syncing with Google Sheets, the application communicates directly with Google servers. For domain-restricted links, the communication happens entirely within your local webview using your current active Google Workspace session. No credentials or sheet data are stored or routed through intermediate APIs.

3. Local Configuration Storage

Sync URLs and cached spreadsheet rows are saved in your local webview's localStorage database to persist your dashboard state. This data is stored strictly on your device.

4. Error Logging

Application runtime error logs are written to your local file system under the application's local log directory to assist in diagnostics. These logs never leave your machine.
